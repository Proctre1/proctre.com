Private by design

No cloud. No bank logins. No one reading your numbers.

Most accounting software keeps your books on someone else's servers. Proctre Ledger keeps them in a single encrypted file that you own.

  • Encrypted company files. Each set of books is one AES-256 encrypted file, autosaved with rolling backups.
  • Nothing leaves your device. No server receives your data, and license keys are checked offline.
  • No sensitive identifiers. No SSN or EIN fields, and account numbers in imported statements are masked to the last 4.
  • Your data is never held hostage. If a license lapses, you can still open, export and back up everything.
