What’s inside

Everything a small business needs to close the books.

One app for the owner’s books and the practice that keeps them, from the first bank import to the locked month.

  • Financial statements

    P&L, balance sheet, cash flow and trial balance, per entity or consolidated with intercompany eliminations.

  • Filing calendar

    Federal and state deadlines by entity type and fiscal year end, moved to the next business day, exportable to your calendar.

  • Tax estimates

    Owner 1040 projections linked to the books, self-employment tax, QBI, safe harbor and a quarterly payment plan.

  • Invoices & receipts

    Send invoices, match payments to bank deposits automatically, and keep receipts linked from your own Drive folder.

  • Month-end close

    A checklist per entity: imports current, nothing uncategorized, accounts reconciled, unusual changes reviewed, month locked.

  • Quick add

    Type “Paid 59.99 to Adobe yesterday with Amex” and it’s booked. Plain-English notes flag late invoices and tax not set aside.

Private by design

  • No cloud.
  • No bank logins.
  • No one reading your numbers.

Encrypted company files. Each set of books is one AES-256 encrypted file, autosaved with rolling backups.

Nothing leaves your device. No server receives your data, and license keys are checked offline.

No sensitive identifiers. No SSN or EIN fields, and account numbers in imported statements are masked to the last 4.

Never held hostage. If a license lapses, you can still open, export and back up everything.
